Frequently asked questions

Clear aligners: your questions.

Your questions about aligners and everyday wear.

Dr. Teeth Family DentalCare that feels like family.

Invisalign is an excellent modern option for most patients, but it’s not for everyone. Aligners generally work best for adults and teens with mild-to-moderate orthodontic issues. Cases that are more complex may require more precise tooth control and movement than a Clear Aligner can offer.

The Invisalign website says their treatment costs anywhere from $3,000–$7,000. And they say that people may qualify for up to $3,000 in help from their insurance company. According to the Consumer Guide for Dentistry, the national average for Invisalign is $3,000–$5,000.

Invisalign Clear Aligners gradually aligns your irregular teeth to achieve a perfect result. It minimizes your discomfort and ensures a beautiful healthy smile. It is not expected to see changes in one to two weeks. Speed of treatment outcome varies among patients. Some patients may begin to notice differences within 10 to 12 weeks.

Who could be a candidate for I nvisalign First clear aligners? As a child grows, their smile grows with them. And even with baby teeth, it may be the perfect time for children ages 6 to 10 to start orthodontic treatment. This is known as Phase 1 treatment.

Tooth shape: Too short or pegged teeth can prevent Invisalign from working properly. Tooth position: If your teeth are too rotated, Invisalign cannot shift them into proper alignment. Large gaps: Even though Invisalign can fix small gaps between the teeth, large gaps may require braces.

Invisalign is a great tooth straightening option for those who want a straighter smile but don’t want to wear metal braces. Invisalign is a great option for people who want to straighten their teeth while they continue with their every day without feeling self-conscious.

Apart from just giving a straight tooth, Invisalign also has the capability to remodel face shape, appearance and profile too. Crooked teeth influences the shape of face whereas an overbite could force your upper lip to protrude. Invisalign also helps to improve the health and appearance of teeth.

Can teens use Invisalign? The simple answer to your question is: yes. When you choose Invisalign to correct your smile, you’ll avoid those feelings of self-consciousness and embarrassment that can come with traditional metal braces.

Invisalign is most effective when it is worn for more than twenty-three hours every day. If a person does not wear the Invisalign aligners for most of their day, they will begin to see slower, and then no results at all. Aligners should only be taken out to eat and to brush and floss the teeth.

Eating and drinking with Invisalign

One of the best things about Invisalign is that your clear aligners are entirely removable, meaning that they can be taken out at mealtimes. This is a massive benefit compared to conventional braces which are permanent and therefore will be left in place while you eat.

Invisalign may not be suitable for you in the following situations: If you still have baby teeth present. if you have had certain teeth extracted. if you have missing teeth, we may not be able to close them.

Most people are candidates for Invisalign, but exceptions to this rule may include the following: Patients who smoke and are unable or unwilling to stop during treatment may not be able to wear Invisalign. Smoking may cause discoloration of the aligners, making them appear stained and unattractive.

Invisalign trays gently nudge teeth into better alignment through micro-movements. The small shifts are spread out over several months or longer, so patients feel minimal discomfort throughout the treatment. … This is why Invisalign recommends that the trays are worn for at least 20 to 22 hours per day.

Clear aligners are a modern and near-invisible method for correcting mild to moderate orthodontic problems. They align teeth just as Braces do, but using a transparent, removable aligner. … In general, orthodontic treatment using clear aligners is faster than with traditional braces.

Eating and drinking with Invisalign

One of the best things about Invisalign is that your clear aligners are entirely removable, meaning that they can be taken out at mealtimes. This is a massive benefit compared to conventional braces which are permanent and therefore will be left in place while you eat.

Can Invisalign clear aligners fix an overbite? Yes, Invisalign clear aligners can fix an overbite or deep bite.
