Our treatments

Clear aligners

A discreet, modern approach to a straighter smile, designed around your everyday life.

Dr. Teeth Family DentalCare that feels like family.

Care that starts with understanding you.

Clear aligners use a series of removable trays to gradually move teeth. A consultation and 3D scanning help our team assess suitability and plan your treatment. Regular reviews help monitor progress throughout your journey.

Discuss your options

Your consultation is a chance to understand the available care and decide on your next step with your dentist.

  • Initial smile assessment
  • 3D scanning and treatment planning
  • Custom aligner fabrication
  • Progress reviews and retention advice

Before your visit

Bring your questions and any information about previous treatment. The team can explain the assessment, appointment stages, and estimated costs before you decide.
