# Homepage treatment feature cards

The homepage includes two cards below the hero treatment navigation, linking to cosmetic dentistry and clear aligner treatment. Both the standalone page and Django template include the section.

Generated using the built-in image generation tool, with a pale blush/lavender studio backdrop, natural South Asian models, and deep plum clothing to match the website palette. These images are illustrative campaign photography, not patient treatment results.

Website assets:

- `static/dental_family/assets/images/theme/cosmetic-smile-focus-plum-v1.webp`: natural smile close-up.
- `static/dental_family/assets/images/theme/clear-aligner-focus-plum-v1.webp`: smiling woman holding a transparent aligner.

Both files are 640 × 960 pixels, exported as quality-87 WebP, and lazy-loaded. The two assets total approximately 100 KB. Styling is in `static/dental_family/css/theme.css` under the treatment stories comment.
