Teeth whitening: your questions.

Whitening options, sensitivity, and what to expect.

Dr. Teeth Family DentalCare that feels like family.

There are many teeth whitening products out there in the market. But every product doesn’t perform in the same way. Some clinics prefer light activated teeth whitening system. They were gold standard for quite a few time. But recently chemically activated teeth whitening systems are being revealed and becoming popular.

Heat and cold sensitivity could develop after teeth whitening. However, this only last for a few days in most people.

Treatment cost varies from clinic to clinic. It depends on materials used, services provided, promotional discounts and specialist care. But the treatment is worthy if you consider the benefits you will get from it.

Using a toothpaste that contains baking soda and hydrogen peroxide can help your teeth to get whiter. There are many teeth whitening toothpaste currently available in the market. Professional Teeth Whitening systems contain different concentrations of hydrogen peroxide.

The active ingredients used in professional Teeth Whitening system is Hydrogen peroxide or Carbamide peroxide. Hydrogen peroxide is the main whitening agent. Carbamide peroxide breaks down to Hydrogen peroxide.
