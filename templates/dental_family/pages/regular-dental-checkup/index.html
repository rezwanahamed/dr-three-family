What Is Regular Dental Checkup?

There are 2 parts of a regular dental visit – check up, or examination and the cleaning. At the dental check up we will check for cavities. X-rays might be taken to detect cavities between your teeth & will scan every tooth to detect caries, plaque & gum disease . Contact us today to reserve an appointment.

What Do We Recommend?

For all of our patients, we offer regular dental checkups. This will allow us to do different diagnostic tests on your teeth over a period of time. So, we could understand your oral health pattern and could diagnose any disease that is about to start. As a very high-tech dental clinic, we can compare your previous scanned photos and x-rays to each other. We recommend you to visit us for a checkup at Dr. Teeth Family Dental, twice per year.

How It Is Done?

We will focus on radio graphic examination with Digital Radiographs. We will also perform Scanning of Dental Caries, Plaque & Gum inflammation. After 6 months on your next visit, we will focus on your clinical periodontal examination. We will gather data of how your gum tissues and bone surrounding your teeth are being maintained. At every time, we will gather intra-oral photographic records to show you and keep a record of your progress. We maintain this standard of care for all patients. This allows us to work seamlessly for your benefit of preventative care. Detecting an issue before it starts is our primary focus.

How Often Do You Need A Dental Checkup?

We recommend that you should have a dental check–up every 6 months, but some people may need more frequent checks. The time between check–ups can vary from 3 months to 1 year. It will depend on how healthy your teeth and gums are and your risk of future diseases.
