What Is Dental Veneer?

We use Emax Veneers and Porcelain Veneers to improve the appearance of your smile. These Veneer are made of thin yet durable lithium-di-silicate that looks just like your natural dental enamel. They’re an ideal choice to cover and protect teeth that are broken, chipped or damaged in any way. We apply Veneers onto the surface of your existing teeth, where they create a natural look and provide long-lasting results. We have extensive experience working with Veneers, so you can expect perfect results on every tooth. Imagine visiting the dentist for just a few hours and walking away with all your dental imperfections corrected. No wonder Hollywood stars get Veneers only.

How Much Do Veneers Cost For Your Teeth?

E-max Veneers and Porcelain Veneers are very expensive material than traditional Composite veneers. E-max is very tough material. They rarely breaks. Veneer cost varies from clinic to clinic depending on expertise of dentist, quality of associated materials & services they offer.

How Long Do Dental Veneers Last?

E-max (lithium-di-silicate) is very tough material. It is very unlikely that your veneer will break. The longevity of E-max veneer depends on mainly veneer design & luting cement quality.

Is Veneer Good For Teeth?

Veneers are a great way to improve your smile & overall facial appearance. If your teeth are broken, discoloured or malformed, veneers are one of the best approach to improve you smile. The amazing part of veneer is they can be made & delivered in two visits only. The colour of E-max veneer rarely changes and they have the real look of natural teeth.

What Is A Veneer For Teeth?

Dental Veneers are very thin, custom made shells of tooth colored materials which are designed to cover the front surface of your teeth to improve your appearance & smile. These shells will be bonded to the front surface of your teeth and they will change your teeth’s color, size & shape.
