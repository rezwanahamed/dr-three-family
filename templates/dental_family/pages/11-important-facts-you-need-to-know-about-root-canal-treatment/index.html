Understanding root canal treatment

A starting point for discussing treatment with your dentist.

Dr. Teeth Family DentalCare that feels like family.

What treatment involves

Root canal treatment removes infected tissue from inside a tooth. The dentist cleans the canals, fills them, and seals the tooth. Local anaesthetic is usually used. Your dentist can explain the approach appropriate for your tooth.

Plan for your appointments

Treatment may involve more than one appointment and a temporary filling between visits. Some teeth also need a crown. Ask which steps are included in your estimate and how follow-up will be arranged.

Questions worth asking

What are my options? How many visits are expected? What restoration will I need afterward? Who should I contact if I have concerns between appointments? Your care team can answer these for your individual situation.
