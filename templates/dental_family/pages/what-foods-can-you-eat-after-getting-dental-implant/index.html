Planning meals after an implant appointment

Useful questions to ask your treating clinician before going home.

Dr. Teeth Family DentalCare that feels like family.

Ask for your own aftercare plan

Before your implant appointment, ask your surgeon for written eating and drinking instructions. Your plan should reflect the treatment you are having and the stage of your care.

Make a practical checklist

Ask which food textures and temperatures are suitable, when you can start eating, and whether you should avoid chewing in a particular area. Check when and how your plan will change.

Keep the clinic’s contact details

Ask who to contact if you are unsure about the instructions or have difficulty following them. Your treating team is the right place for advice specific to your procedure.
