Planning your cosmetic dental consultation

Bring your goals, your questions, and a clear idea of what matters to you.

Dr. Teeth Family DentalCare that feels like family.

Start with your goals

Think about what you would like to discuss: tooth colour, spacing, shape, or your gum line. A consultation is a chance to explain those goals and understand which options the dentist would recommend considering.

Explore the options

Our clinic lists whitening, veneers, cosmetic bonding, gum reshaping, restorations, and orthodontic care among its services. Ask which options fit your needs, what each involves, and whether alternatives are available.

Understand the commitment

Discuss the proposed appointments, costs, maintenance, and expected limitations before deciding. Ask for a written treatment plan and take time to consider it.
