Your child’s first dental visit

Make an early visit feel familiar, positive, and comfortable.

Dr. Teeth Family DentalCare that feels like family.

Start with a friendly introduction

NHS guidance recommends a first dental visit when milk teeth appear, or before a child’s first birthday. An early appointment gives your child a chance to get used to the setting and lets you ask about caring for their teeth.

Bring your questions

Tell the dentist about your child’s brushing routine, anything you have noticed, and any worries about the appointment. Ask how to make brushing easier and when to arrange the next checkup.

Keep it positive

Talk about the visit in a relaxed way. Let our team know if your child is nervous so we can discuss how to make the introduction more comfortable.
