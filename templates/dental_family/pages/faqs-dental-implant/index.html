Dental implants: your questions.

Planning, suitability, and caring for your implant.

Dr. Teeth Family DentalCare that feels like family.

It is possible sometimes to place Dental Implants on the same appointment of teeth removal. Usually, three months of healing time is needed before Implant placement. If you wait for a very long time after tooth removal, there will be loss of bone. It will make Implant placement more difficult in future.

Success rate of Dental Implant is very high. In general, Dental Implants have a success rate of up to 98%. If proper care is taken, Dental Implants can last a lifetime.

Dental Implants are considered with very high success rate. Still some people may experience failure. Research shows that about 5 to 10 percent of Dental Implants may fail.

Dental Implants are “safe”. But dentists should always have a look at potential risks. If there is any gum disease or tooth decay, they need to treated first before placing Dental Implant.

Dental Implants are expensive treatment. You might compare Implants with Bridges and Dentures in terms of cost, comfort, and durability. Dental Implant is by far the most advanced teeth replacement treatment. Considering the cost of Implant, specialist care needed & benefits you will get, definitely it is worthy.
